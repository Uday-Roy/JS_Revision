Cookie Demo

Welcome to the Cookie Demo!

A cookie is a small piece of data that is stored on the user's computer by the web browser while browsing a website. It is used to remember information about the user, such as their preferences, login status, or shopping cart contents.

This is a simple example of how to use cookies in JavaScript.

When you visit this page for the first time, a cookie will be created to store your username and password.

On subsequent visits, the script will check for the cookie and welcome you back if it exists.
